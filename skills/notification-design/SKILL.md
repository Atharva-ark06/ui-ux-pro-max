# Notification Design
1. Identify whether information is urgent, important but nonurgent, or informational.
2. Choose a channel and interruption level appropriate to the user's context.
3. State what happened and what action, if any, is needed.
4. Avoid duplicate notifications for the same event.
5. Provide a destination that matches the notification's promise.
6. Respect preferences, quiet hours, and permission denial.
7. Make status available in-app when push delivery fails.
8. Measure useful engagement and dismissals, not notification volume alone.

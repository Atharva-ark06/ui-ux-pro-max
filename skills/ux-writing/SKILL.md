# UX Writing
1. Identify what the user needs to know at this exact step.
2. Lead with the outcome or action, not internal system terminology.
3. Use specific verbs on buttons; avoid vague labels such as “Submit” when the outcome can be named.
4. Keep labels consistent across the same journey.
5. Explain consequences before destructive or irreversible actions.
6. Write error messages with a cause when known and a recovery step.
7. Avoid jargon, unnecessary exclamation marks, and promises the product cannot guarantee.
8. Review text in context, including narrow screens, localization, and screen-reader output.

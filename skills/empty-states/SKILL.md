# Empty States
Identify why the state is empty before choosing the message.
- **First use:** explain what belongs here and how to begin.
- **No results:** summarize the search/filter context and offer a recovery action.
- **User cleared data:** confirm the result and explain how to add items again.
- **Unavailable data:** distinguish temporary failure from a genuinely empty collection.
Use a concise heading, one useful explanation, and one primary next step. Avoid decorative illustrations that delay understanding. Preserve the user's query and filters when they retry. Test empty states at narrow widths and with assistive technology.

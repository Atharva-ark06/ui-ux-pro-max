# Checkout Flow
1. Show total cost, taxes, shipping, and recurring charges before final confirmation.
2. Let users understand progress and return to earlier steps without losing data.
3. Offer guest checkout when appropriate and defer account creation if it is not required.
4. Label payment and address fields clearly; explain accepted formats.
5. Provide delivery, refund, privacy, and support information near relevant decisions.
6. Prevent duplicate charges during retries and show a clear transaction result.
7. Make validation errors specific and preserve valid form input.
8. Test keyboard flow, mobile entry, slow networks, and failed payment recovery.

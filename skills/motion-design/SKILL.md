# Motion Design
1. Give every animation a communication purpose.
2. Keep essential feedback immediate and avoid delaying user control.
3. Respect reduced-motion preferences and provide simpler alternatives for nonessential motion.
4. Avoid flashing patterns and large parallax effects that can cause discomfort.
5. Animate properties that can be rendered efficiently where possible.
6. Ensure motion does not obscure text, focus, or important controls.
7. Test on lower-powered devices and with reduced motion enabled.
8. Do not use animation as the only signal that state changed.

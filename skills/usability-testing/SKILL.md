# Usability Testing
1. Turn product assumptions into task-based research questions.
2. Recruit participants representative of the target audience and disclose limitations.
3. Write realistic scenarios without revealing the intended interface path.
4. Ask participants to think aloud, but avoid coaching them through difficulties.
5. Record task success, time, errors, hesitation, and participant explanations.
6. Separate observed behavior from moderator interpretation.
7. Cluster repeated problems and rank them by severity and frequency.
8. Retest fixes with new tasks or participants where practical.
Do not treat a small convenience sample as statistically representative. Protect participant data and obtain appropriate consent.

# Trust Signal Review
1. Identify decisions where users need evidence before proceeding.
2. Check whether pricing, terms, contact options, privacy practices, and limitations are easy to find.
3. Ensure testimonials, certifications, and metrics are accurate and attributable.
4. Explain why sensitive information is requested.
5. Avoid fabricated scarcity, misleading badges, and unsupported security claims.
6. Make errors and service limitations transparent.
7. Check consistency between marketing promises and actual flow behavior.
8. Verify every trust claim with a reliable source before publication.

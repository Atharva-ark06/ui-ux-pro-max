# UX Research Synthesis Workflow

Use this workflow to turn interview notes or usability observations into grounded findings.

## Steps
1. Define the research question and participant/context limits.
2. Organize notes by observed behavior, direct quote, and researcher interpretation.
3. Group repeated patterns without erasing contradictory evidence.
4. Separate findings from hypotheses and recommendations.
5. Prioritize by user impact, frequency, and confidence.
6. Link each finding to supporting evidence.
7. Propose follow-up research for unresolved questions.

## Reporting safeguards
Do not invent quotes, overstate representativeness, or expose identifying participant details. An observation from a small sample is not automatically a population-level conclusion.

## Deliverable
Summarize key findings, evidence, confidence, product implications, limitations, and next steps.

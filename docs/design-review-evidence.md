## Review principles
- Separate direct observations from hypotheses about why they happened.
- Describe the user impact, not just the visual difference.
- Include device, viewport, browser, and relevant state when reporting a defect.
- Prefer a specific, testable recommendation over vague advice such as “make it cleaner.”
- Recheck the finding after the change and note whether the original issue is resolved.

Severity should reflect impact on the task, how often it occurs, and how many users are affected. Do not label a cosmetic issue a blocker unless it prevents a core task.

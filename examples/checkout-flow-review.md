# Example: Checkout Flow Review

This is a hypothetical checklist, not an audit of a live store.

## Review questions
- Is the full payable amount visible before final confirmation?
- Can a user understand delivery, tax, recurring billing, and refund conditions?
- Can they correct shipping and payment details without losing work?
- Are guest checkout and account creation choices explained?
- Do payment failures state whether a charge occurred?
- Are retry controls protected against accidental duplicate submission?
- Can keyboard and mobile users complete every step?
